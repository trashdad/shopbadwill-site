ShopBadwill privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

ShopBadwill is a personal browser extension. It has no servers of its own and collects nothing for its developer.

What it stores

Searches, filters, matched listings, snipe settings and preferences are stored in the browser's own extension storage on your computer.

Google data

With your permission, ShopBadwill uses one Google scope, calendar.app.created. It can only see and change calendars it creates itself. It uses them to add auction reminders and to keep its settings in step between your own browsers. It cannot read your other calendars or events. Google data is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. ShopBadwill's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

The extension talks to shopgoodwill.com to search and bid for you, and, if you set one up, to the ntfy server you choose for notifications. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Removing your data

Uninstall the extension to delete its local data. Remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the calendars it created in Google Calendar.
